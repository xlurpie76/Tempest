# Tempest Hotel

A hotel reservation system that allows guests to browse available rooms, select their preferred dates, provide contact information, and complete their room bookings through an organized and user-friendly interface.

## Tempest Hotel Reservation System

**Actors:** (not yet implemented im pretty sure?)
- Guests 
- Hotel Staff
- Admin

**Use Cases:**
- Search and browse available rooms
- Select and book a hotel room
- Choose check-in and check-out dates
- Enter guest contact information
- Provide payment details
- Generate and view booking receipts

**Sequence Idea:**

Room Selection → Date Selection → Contact Information Form → Payment Details → Reservation Receipt

**Class Concepts:**
- **Room List** – Displays available hotel rooms in a scrollable panel, allowing guests to browse and select their preferred accommodation.
- **Calendar** – Allows guests to select their desired check-in and check-out dates.
- **Contact Information Form** – Collects the guest's necessary personal and contact details for the reservation.
- **Payment Details** – Handles the entry of payment information needed to complete the booking.
- **Receipt** – Displays the reservation summary and payment information after completing the booking.

## Project Description

Tempest Hotel aims to simplify the hotel reservation process by providing a step-by-step booking experience. Guests can browse rooms, choose their preferred stay dates, submit their contact information, and enter payment details. Once the process is completed, the system generates a receipt containing the reservation details as confirmation of the booking.

The system is designed to make hotel reservations more convenient, organized, and accessible for both guests and hotel personnel.
